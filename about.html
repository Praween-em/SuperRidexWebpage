About

Built so every driver can compete for the next ride

SUPER RIDEX is an accessibility assistive app. It uses Android Accessibility Service to help eligible drivers activate Accept on booking and delivery apps when they cannot press it reliably on their own.

Who it’s for

Drivers who face motor, coordination, vision-related timing, or similar challenges often miss Accept windows — and lose income. SUPER RIDEX exists to close that gap with lawful, purpose-built assistance.

Accessibility first

Permissions are requested only so the assistive Accept function can work when a request appears.

Driver dignity

You stay in control of your shift, your platform apps, and your compliance with local laws and partner terms.

Practical earning

More fair chances at Accept means more chances to ride, earn, and build a steadier day.

Mission

Ride. Earn. Repeat.

We believe disability should not decide who gets the next trip. SUPER RIDEX gives professional drivers an assistive edge on Accept screens — so skill and hustle matter more than finger speed.
